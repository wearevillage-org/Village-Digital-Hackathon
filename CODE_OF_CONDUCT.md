# Code of Conduct

Village is a queer community space. This weekend, and in this repository, we want everyone to feel welcome and safe.

## How we treat each other

- **Be curious, not judgmental.**
- **Make room for different voices and experiences.**
- **Use people's names and pronouns correctly.**
- **No discriminatory language.**
- **If something hurtful happens, say so kindly so we can all learn.** You can also tell us, and we'll help.

## What we don't accept

Harassment, hate speech, unwanted sexual attention or content, and intimidation. Please also ask before taking photos of people, and keep real personal data of members out of the code, the chat and the demos.

## Where this applies

At the event, in the hackathon chat, and in this repository (issues, pull requests, comments).

## If something happens

Talk to any organiser, in person or privately in the chat, or write to **info@wearevillage.org**. We will listen, take it seriously, and act on it. Organisers may ask someone to change their behaviour or to leave the event.

Thank you for helping us make this a good space. 💛
